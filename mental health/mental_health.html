Need Immediate Support?

If you are struggling or need someone to talk to right now, these resources are available 24/7.

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

Call or text 988 for free, confidential crisis and emotional support in the United States.

Spanish support is available by calling 988 and pressing 2, or by texting AYUDA to 988.

Visit 988 Lifeline

Crisis Text Line

Text HOME to 741741 to connect with a trained volunteer Crisis Counselor.

Spanish speakers can text HOLA to 741741.

Visit Crisis Text Line

If there is an immediate medical emergency or someone is in immediate physical danger, call 911.
